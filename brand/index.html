Archivo / Display

Aa 0123

Archivo supplies the headlines and the outlined wordmark. Inter Tight handles reading and labels. Use weights 570–620 for display and 400–500 for text. The wordmark is optically spaced artwork at weight 650 and width 94.

Both families use the SIL Open Font License: Archivo and Inter Tight. Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, and Arial are the fallbacks.

03 / A sense of place

The view from here.

The place is present in the structure: Michigan Modern’s economy, the Guardian’s material warmth, the Penobscot’s clear silhouette, the Mackinac Bridge’s sense of connection, and the open space of Lake Michigan.

The original Belle Isle drawing remains the companion illustration: Livingstone Lighthouse, a freighter, and open water. Keep it wide, on covers or quiet transitions. The block and wordmark stand on their own at smaller sizes.

04 / In use

Made for the everyday.

The same spacing, colors, and proportions carry into a letter, an email, or a profile. Each file below uses the current identity.

05 / The system

A small set of decisions, carried through.

Clear margins, square edges, and the same solid block. The parent company stays recognizable while each venture keeps its own identity.

The voice

Say what helps.
Show what is real.

“We get to know your business. Then we build what helps.”

Plain words, specific work, and accurate credit. Lead with what changes for the customer. Keep client and venture identities intact.

Reference points: Cranbrook’s Michigan Modern, the Guardian Building, the Mackinac Bridge, and Knoll’s graphic history. The compositions are original; historic artwork is not reproduced.
