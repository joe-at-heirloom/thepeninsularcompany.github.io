Privacy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Inkblotter does not collect any data about you. There is no account, no sign-in, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Inkblotter is published by The Peninsular Company, LLC, a Michigan limited liability company based in Detroit.

What stays on your device

  • The ink you drop, the blot it makes and what you type about it stay on your iPhone or iPad. Inkblotter doesn’t save them: starting a new blot clears the last one.
  • What you type about the ten plates in The Ten Plates stays on your device too, and is gone when you close the book.
  • Readings are written on your device. Where Apple Intelligence is available, Inkblotter uses Apple’s on-device model; elsewhere it uses text built into the app. Nothing you type is sent to us or to anyone else.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a reading, you choose where the image goes using the iOS share sheet. We never see what you share or where it goes.

Buying the app

You buy Inkblotter from Apple, through the App Store. Apple takes the payment and does not give us your name, your Apple Account or your payment details. Apple tells us how many copies were sold and in which countries. If you have chosen in your device’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple also sends us crash reports and usage figures that do not identify you.

If you write to us

When you email inkblotter@thepeninsularcompany.com, we receive your email address, your name if your email includes it, your message and any attachments. We use them to answer you and to keep a record of our conversation. Our email hosting provider stores these messages for us. We do not sell them.

We keep email for three years after our last contact. You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it: write to the same address.

This website

These pages are part of thepeninsularcompany.com, a static site that sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking, advertising, forms or outside scripts and fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address and the details of your request for security and operations. The Peninsular Company’s privacy policy covers the website.

Children

Inkblotter collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If any of this changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
