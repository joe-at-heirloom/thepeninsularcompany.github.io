The ink blot test you fold

On the App Store October 23, 2026

Close your phone to press the ink.

Inkblotter turns iPhone Duo into a sheet of paper folded in half. Drop ink on the right-hand page, close the phone to press it, and open it again to find a mirror-image blot that nobody has seen before.

Then say what you see. A Victorian Examiner, a kindly gentleman-scientist of the 1880s, writes your reading in his casebook: a remark on your blot, then what each part of your answer says about you, and why.

The fold, in six figures

From the cover to the reading

Also inside

The Ten Plates

The ten inkblots Hermann Rorschach published in Bern in 1921, shown one at a time. Say the first thing you see, then turn the page: what most people saw in that plate, how he would have marked your answer, what later examiners claimed for it, and whether any of that held up.

After the tenth plate comes a scoring sheet tallied his way, with a plain note on how a century of testing treated it. It is a tour of a famous experiment, not a test of you.

The fold

The phone is the paper

As the hinge closes, the print spreads out from the spine the way ink does between two sheets. Open it again and strands of wet ink stretch across the crease and snap, with haptics and synthesized paper sounds timed to the hinge.

The reading

A century of lore, read kindly

Every reading explains itself, grounded in a review of inkblot research from Kerner’s 1857 klecksography to the 2013 meta-analysis of Rorschach scoring. It leans on what held up, like movement and how you connect things, and it never diagnoses. Readings are written on the phone; where Apple Intelligence is available it adds the Examiner’s own verdict. It’s a parlor game.

The book

Made like a Victorian book

Bodoni type, pages in book proportions, endpapers marbled the way a marbler does it, by throwing drops of color that push each other aside, and ink whose edges measure as rough as the edges of Rorschach’s own 1921 plates. On other iPhones and iPad, a Fold button turns the page.
