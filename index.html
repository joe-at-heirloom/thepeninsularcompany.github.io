02 How we help

We get to know your business. Then we build what helps.

Start with what you want to improve. We’ll learn how your business works, find a useful place to begin, and take it from an idea to something your team can use.

  1. 01

    Understand the business

    Get to know your customers, your team, and how work gets done. Look for what takes too much time and where there’s room to grow.

  2. 02

    Choose what will help

    Work out what’s worth improving first, with a clear scope and a budget that makes sense for your business.

  3. 03

    Design, build & refine

    Build the solution, put it to work, and improve it around the people using it.

  • Branding & websites

    Give your business an identity and a website that reflect the quality of your work, explain what makes it different, and help customers take the next step.

  • Software & automation

    Cut the hours spent on spreadsheets, duplicate data entry, and disconnected tools. Improve what you use or build something that fits the way your team works.

  • Marketing & growth

    Make it easier for the right customers to find and choose you, with clear positioning, useful content, and campaigns built around your goals.

A clear scope, a sensible investment, and a useful first improvement you can build on.

03 For auction houses

Auction technology & advisory

Bespoke consulting for auction houses, from catalog creation to the final bid. Practical technology and thoughtful marketing that support your specialists, strengthen consignor and bidder relationships, and give each lot the attention it deserves.

  • Catalog creation & automation

    Streamline image handling, organize lot data, and automate catalog preparation with AI-assisted description drafts and specialist review built into the process.

  • Lot presentation & sale strategy

    Tailor research, photography, descriptions, and promotion to each lot and its likely collectors, with the aim of stronger bidding and better hammer results.

  • Consignor development

    Help the right consignors discover your specialties through focused positioning, targeted campaigns, and a simpler path from inquiry to consignment.

  • Bidder discovery & engagement

    Reach new collectors through search and targeted outreach. Help your existing audience find lots that match their interests through personalized recommendations and relevant sale communications.

  • Websites & auction platforms

    Design new websites, improve the online bidding experience, and select, integrate, or build auction platforms around your house’s needs.

  • Operations & efficiency

    Connect cataloging, marketing, client records, and reporting. Reduce duplicate entry and repetitive tasks so your team has more time for the work that needs its expertise.

Led by Joe Lint, drawing on hands-on experience in auction technology, cataloging workflows, and marketing. Every engagement is shaped around your team, your specialties, and the way you work.
