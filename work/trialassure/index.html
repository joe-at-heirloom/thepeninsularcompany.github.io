03 · Life sciences

TrialAssure

Making complex software clear to the people who need it.

Scope
Branding · Website design & development · Marketing input
Role & relationship
Brand and website work by Joe Lint for Lotus International’s sister company, with input on marketing and messaging.

The business context

TrialAssure serves teams working on medical writing, anonymization, and clinical trial disclosure. Visitors bring different responsibilities and need to understand where the platform fits into their work.

The approach

The website organizes the offering around the work people do, supported by product illustrations, demonstrations, and routes for different teams. The brand gives a broad technical offering a consistent presentation.

A closer look

  • Give the platform a structure

    Medical writing, anonymization, and disclosure each have a clear place in the product story.

  • Explain through examples

    Illustrations and demonstrations help visitors understand the offering before requesting a demo.

  • Meet different audiences

    Navigation by team and need connects visitors with relevant information.
