01 · Contract manufacturing

Lotus International

Making a complex manufacturing business easier to understand.

Scope
Branding · Website design & development · Marketing
Role & relationship
In-house work by Joe Lint as Business Development & Marketing Specialist at Lotus International.

The business context

A prospective customer needs to understand what Lotus can take on, how its capabilities fit together, and whether it is the right partner for a particular product.

The approach

The website gives that breadth a structure: capabilities, industries, facility information, and a guided path to an inquiry. The brand and content connect the company’s manufacturing expertise to the questions buyers bring.

A closer look

  • Find the right fit

    Capability and industry pages offer different ways into the same business.

  • Make expertise visible

    Facility imagery and quality credentials bring the operation into the sales conversation.

  • Start a useful conversation

    A guided inquiry gathers product, service, and volume information to help frame the next step.
